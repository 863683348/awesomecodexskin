Character skins are having a moment in the Codex ecosystem. This week's wave brings the **Naraka Wuxia series** — a full set of themes themed around the Naraka: Bladepoint universe — plus Three Kingdoms, Journey to the West, and cultivation-novel inspired entries.

## The Naraka series

The [Naraka Wuxia](/skins/naraka-wanxiang-cangdi-fourfold-axis/) family is the headliner — a cohesive set of dark, atmospheric themes named after the game's clans and locations:

- [Cangdi Fourfold Axis](/skins/naraka-wanxiang-cangdi-fourfold-axis/)
- [Five Aspects](/skins/naraka-wanxiang-five-aspects/)
- [Kuishi Mountain Seal](/skins/naraka-wanxiang-kuishi-mountain-seal/)
- [Minghuo Abyssal Tide](/skins/naraka-wanxiang-minghuo-abyssal-tide/)
- [Shuojin Thunder Forge](/skins/naraka-wanxiang-shuojin-thunder-forge/)
- [Yuyan Ember Prison](/skins/naraka-wanxiang-yuyan-ember-prison/)

What's notable: the series shares a design language — deep dark backgrounds, martial-arts inspired accent colors, high text contrast — so it's one of the few theme *families* in the index where every member feels like part of the same collection.

## The literary wave

Alongside Naraka, the index picked up classic Chinese literary themes:

- [Three Kingdoms](/skins/three-kingdoms/) — the classic trio of heroes as a palette.
- [Journey to the West](/skins/journey-to-west/) — bright and adventurous.
- [Han Li Mortal Path](/skins/han-li-mortal-path/) — a cultivation-novel inspired dark theme.
- [Tianhai Vajra Thunder Temple](/skins/tianhai-vajra-thunder-temple/) and a few more in the same spiritual-fantasy lane.

## Why character themes work in a workspace

The good character skins don't just slap a portrait on a palette — they translate the character's identity into color psychology: bold reds for Three Kingdoms warriors, cool teals for the Thunder Forge, ember-orange for prison themes. The result is a workspace with personality that stays readable. [Shuojin Thunder Forge](/skins/naraka-wanxiang-shuojin-thunder-forge/) is a great example — loud identity, still a functional dark theme.
