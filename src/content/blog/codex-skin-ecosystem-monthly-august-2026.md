Time for the monthly ecosystem check. The Codex skin world is still young, and a month in this space moves fast. Here's what happened and where it's heading.

## The numbers

- **Index grew from 26 to 99 skins** in under a month — a 4x jump driven by community repo imports.
- **Eight curated categories** now have coverage: dark, light, anime, neon, nature, retro, pastel, terminal.
- **Engine options are stable at five** real choices — prompt engines, desktop managers, curated galleries, CLI tools, and TUI managers.

## The big trends

**1. Character themes are booming.** Anime, game, and literary skins (HeiGeAi's Dragon Ball batch, the Naraka series, Three Kingdoms) are the fastest-growing slice of new entries. It's early for Codex, but if editor theme history is any guide, personality skins are here to stay.

**2. Formats are consolidating.** The ecosystem is converging on two practical paths: prompt presets for speed, structured files (tmtheme-family) for portability. The [format guide](/blog/codex-skin-format-ecosystem/) explains the tradeoffs.

**3. The "other" bucket is shrinking.** As curation improves, skins that were dumped into the catch-all category are getting real homes — which makes the [skin index](/skins/) actually browsable.

**4. Terminal integration is maturing.** The CLI ports (Tokyo Night, Monokai Stone, Solarized) prove that terminal-first themes migrate cleanly into Codex — see the [terminal sync guide](/blog/codex-skin-terminal-sync/) for the workflow.

## What's next

- **More curation, fewer duplicates.** The index is tightening up near-duplicate slugs and mislabeled entries.
- **Best-of coverage for every category.** The [dark](/blog/best-dark-codex-skins/) and [anime](/blog/best-anime-codex-skins/) roundups are live; light, terminal, neon, and the rest are on the way.
- **More depth content.** The blog is moving past "what is a skin" into comparison and workflow territory — engines, formats, eye-care, accessibility.

## How to stay current

- Watch the [skin index](/skins/) — new entries land as soon as they're curated.
- Read the category roundups to find the good stuff fast.
- If you make skins, the [submit page](/submit/) is open.

The ecosystem is at the stage where every month changes the map. This time last month the index had 26 skins; next month the story will be different again. The community's momentum — new repos, new formats, new themes — is the real headline. Check back next month for the September edition.
