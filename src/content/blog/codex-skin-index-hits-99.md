The Codex Skin Index crossed 99 indexed skins this week — and the single biggest contributor is the Wangnov open-source collection, which now accounts for more than half of everything in the catalog.

## What changed this week

- **99 skins live**, up from 26 a few weeks ago.
- **Wangnov/awesome-codex-skins passed 50 skins** — the largest single source in the index, spanning dark, light, terminal, and character themes.
- **Category coverage completed** for the eight curated groups: dark-midnight, light-airy, anime-pop, neon-cyberpunk, nature-earthy, retro-vintage, pastel-soft, and mono-terminal.
- **Data cleanup** removed 16 non-skin entries (tutorial pages and stray markdown that had been imported as skins) — the catalog now contains actual themes only.

## The Wangnov collection, in context

The [Wangnov awesome-codex-skins](https://github.com/Wangnov/awesome-codex-skins) repo is the closest thing Codex has to a community theme market. Its 50+ skins in this index include:

- Classic palettes like [Monokai Stone](/skins/monokai-stone/) and [Gothic Void Expedition](/skins/gothic-void-expedition/).
- Light and airy options like [Amber Dusk](/skins/amber-dusk/).
- Cultural and fantasy themes like [Celestial Court](/skins/celestial-court/).

All of them install the same way — the [install guide](/blog/how-to-install-codex-skins/) covers every method.

## What's coming

- **More community imports** from the other major sources (HeiGeAi, the anime and game-themed repos).
- **Better categorization** of the "other" bucket — skins that don't fit the curated eight yet.
- **Per-category best-of roundups**, so you don't have to browse 99 pages to find a good dark theme.

## How to browse the new skins

The [skin index](/skins/) now has a category navigation bar — jump straight to dark, light, anime, neon, or terminal themes instead of scrolling the whole catalog. Each skin's page still shows its install format, palette, and compatible platform.

The 99-skin milestone is a nice round number, but the real signal is the ecosystem's growth: new repos, new authors, new formats every week. The index will keep tracking it. If you've published a Codex skin, the [submit page](/submit/) is the way to get it listed.
