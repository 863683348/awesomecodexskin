# Best Light Codex Skins for Daytime Coding

Working during the day means your screen brightness matches ambient light. A dark theme can cause glare and eye strain in well-lit environments. Light Codex skins are designed for daytime productivity.

## Why Light Themes for Daytime?

Light themes reduce eye strain when your environment is bright. They mimic paper — something our eyes are evolved to read. If you code near a window or in a well-lit office, light themes are your friend.

## FAQ

**Do light themes hurt my eyes?** Only if your environment is dark. Match your theme to your lighting.

**Can I switch between light and dark?** Yes — use Codex Skin Studio to toggle based on time of day.

**Which is better for long coding sessions?** It depends on your environment. Natural light favors light themes.
