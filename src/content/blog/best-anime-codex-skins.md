Work doesn't have to look like a corporate IDE. Anime and pop-culture skins bring personality to Codex Desktop — and because they're just color palettes plus a preview image, they're as easy to switch as any other theme.

## 1. Hatsune Miku — blue-green vocaloid energy

The most installed anime skin in the index. Teal-blues on a clean base with a hint of vocaloid personality.

```text
Codex, apply the 'Hatsune Miku' skin — blue-green vocaloid energy for my workspace.
```

- **Mood:** Anime & Pop · energetic · **2,500+ installs**
- [View skin](/skins/hatsune-miku/)

## 2. Cartethyia, Wind-Tide Sanctum — ethereal fantasy

A game-character skin from the Codex Skin Manager built-in collection. Airy, ethereal tones with a fantasy edge.

```text
In Codex Skin Manager, apply the built-in 'Cartethyia, Wind-Tide Sanctum' theme to your workspace.
```

- **Mood:** Anime & Pop · ethereal
- [View skin](/skins/cartethyia/)

## 3. Dilraba — playful celebrity concept

A fan-made celebrity concept skin from codex-skin.dev. Playful and light, with a pop-culture twist.

```text
On codex-skin.dev, copy the 'Dilraba' install prompt into Codex to apply this fan concept skin.
```

- **Mood:** Anime & Pop · playful
- [View skin](/skins/dilraba/)

## 4. Asuka (Evangelion) — mecha character energy

A character-inspired palette with the bold, confident energy of its source material. Part of a growing Eva family in the index.

- **Mood:** Anime & Pop
- [View skin](/skins/asuka-eva02/)

## 5. Sakura Pink Dawn — soft pastel alternative

Not strictly anime, but the go-to choice when you want pastel-soft vibes that pair well with anime wallpapers.

```text
Codex, switch my workspace to the 'Sakura Pink Dawn' engine preset — a soft sakura-pink theme.
```

- **Mood:** Pastel & Soft · gentle
