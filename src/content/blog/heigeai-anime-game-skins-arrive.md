The [HeiGeAi heige-codex-skin-studio](https://github.com/HeiGeAi/heige-codex-skin-studio) repository delivered 12 new skins to the index this week, and if you're an anime or gaming fan, this batch is for you.

## The full lineup

The HeiGeAi collection covers the biggest fandoms in one sweep:

- **Dragon Ball** — [Super Saiyan](/skins/heige-dragon-ball-super-saiyan/) and the [Nimbus](/skins/heige-dragon-ball-nimbus/) cloud theme.
- **Naruto** — [Naruto](/skins/heige-naruto/) brings orange-to-black energy to your workspace.
- **Genshin Impact** — two moods: [Genshin Dawn](/skins/heige-genshin-dawn/) (bright) and [Genshin Night](/skins/heige-genshin-night/) (dark).
- **Hatsune Miku** — [Miku Future](/skins/heige-miku-future/) and [Miku 488137](/skins/heige-miku-488137/), the teal vocaloid palette in two takes.
- **Wuthering Waves**, **Love and Deepspace**, and a few more from the anime-adjacent world.
- **Honorable mention:** [Dalao Smoke](/skins/heige-dalao-smoke/) — a moody dark theme that doesn't need a fandom to be great.

## What makes these worth installing

Anime skins are usually more than a palette swap. The HeiGeAi batch treats each theme as a character: accent colors pulled from the character's signature look, backgrounds tuned to the scene's mood, and readable syntax colors layered on top. [Genshin Night](/skins/heige-genshin-night/), for instance, is a genuinely usable dark theme, not just a fandom sticker.

## Where they fit in the index

These land in the **anime-pop** category alongside the existing character skins — [Hatsune Miku](/skins/hatsune-miku/), [Dilraba](/skins/dilraba/), and the rest. If anime isn't your thing, the collection still adds value: several of the HeiGeAi themes are strong dark options that could easily live in a "just good themes" list.

## How to install

Every skin in this batch uses the standard install methods from the [install guide](/blog/how-to-install-codex-skins/) — copy the prompt or download the theme file, depending on the skin's format. No special setup needed.
