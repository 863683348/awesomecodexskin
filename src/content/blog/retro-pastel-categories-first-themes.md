The two thinnest categories in the index just got their first real attention. **Retro-vintage** and **pastel-soft** were nearly empty; this week's imports give both a proper starting lineup.

## Retro-vintage: the QQ2007 revival

The retro wave is led by a piece of internet history: the QQ2007 theme — a nostalgia-heavy palette based on the classic 2007-era look. Two entries landed:

- [QQ2007 Retro](/skins/qq2007-retro/) — the faithful recreation.
- [HeiGeAi QQ2007 Retro](/skins/heige-qq2007-retro/) — a variant with slightly adjusted tones.

Paired with [Solarized](/skins/solarized/), which carries its own old-school credentials, the retro category now has three distinct options instead of being a footnote.

## Pastel-soft: gentle themes arrive

The pastel corner also filled in with a clean, soft trio:

- [Sakura Pink Dawn](/skins/sakura-pink-dawn/) — the cherry-blossom pastel, immediately the category's standout.
- [Pink Custom](/skins/pink-custom/) — a softer, customizable pink.
- [Hashimoto Arata](/skins/hashimoto-arata/) — a gentle, low-saturation option for people who want pastel without pink.

For light-theme fans, pastel is the friendliest entry point — low contrast by design, easy on the eyes in daylight. The [light vs dark guide](/blog/codex-light-vs-dark-skins/) explains why that matters.

## Why filling sparse categories matters

An index is only as good as its weakest category. When a category has one or two entries, people assume the ecosystem doesn't support that style and move on. Now that retro and pastel have real options, they're searchable, comparable, and discoverable — which is the whole point of a curated index.

## What this means for browsing

- The [skin index](/skins/) category nav now has meaningful choices in all eight curated groups.
- The pastel and retro themes pair well with the [accessibility guide](/blog/codex-skin-accessibility-contrast/) — low-saturation skins are often the easiest to check against contrast requirements.
- Both categories are still open for more submissions. If you make a retro or pastel theme, the [submit page](/submit/) is the way in.

## Coming next

The remaining thin spot is the "other" bucket — the catch-all for community skins that don't map cleanly to the curated eight. Curation is working through it in batches, and each batch adds more depth to the real categories. Watch the [skin index](/skins/) for the next update.
