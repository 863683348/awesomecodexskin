# Best Pastel-Soft Codex Skins: Soft Colors That Stay Readable

Pastel-soft Codex skins trade harsh contrast for gentle, low-saturation palettes. If you want a calm workspace without the eye fatigue of pure white or the heaviness of full dark mode, this roundup is for you. I tested the three pastel-soft skins in the library and picked the ones that hold up during real coding sessions. The best pastel-soft Codex skins balance a soft look with enough contrast to keep text legible.

## Why Pick a Pastel-Soft Skin

A pastel skin is not just "pink." The good ones keep syntax colors distinct while softening the background. I like them for late-night work when a bright theme feels like staring into a lamp. They also photograph well for screenshots, which matters if you share your setup.

## The Three Pastel-Soft Picks

### 1. Hashimoto Arata
A soft light theme with rose tones. Fei-Away built it as a machine-tested preset, so the highlights and shadows stay balanced. It is a solid default if you want light mode that does not glare.

### 2. Pink Custom
A gentle pink gradient made for late-night coding in the Codex Dream Skin engine. It leans dreamy but keeps the editor chrome readable. If you write in the evening, this one feels easy on the eyes.

### 3. Sakura Pink Dawn
A soft sakura-pink abstract preset (MIT) from the Codex Cidala Tiger Skin repo. It is the most decorative of the three, with a subtle abstract pattern rather than a flat fill.

## Pastel-Soft Skins at a Glance

| Skin | Base tone | Best for | Engine |
|------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Hashimoto Arata | Rose light | Daytime light mode | Codex Desktop |
| Pink Custom | Pink gradient | Late-night coding | Dream Skin |
| Sakura Pink Dawn | Sakura abstract | Decorative setups | Cidala Tiger |

## How to Install

1. Open Codex.
2. Run `/theme hashimoto-arata` (swap the slug for pink-custom or sakura-pink-dawn).
3. Adjust your monitor brightness if the pastel tone feels too light for the room.

## Pairing Pastel With a Focused Workflow

Soft colors do not mean a soft setup. Keep your keybindings, AI assists, and multi-file navigation. The skin only changes what you see. Some people run a pastel desktop skin by day and a mono or dark skin by night, switching with one command.

## FAQ

**Are pastel-soft Codex skins safe to install?**
Yes. They are theme files that only change colors and window chrome. They do not edit your code or run scripts.

**Will a pastel skin hurt readability?**
Only if the contrast is too low. The three picks above keep syntax colors distinct. If a pastel theme feels washed out, lower your screen brightness or pick Hashimoto Arata, which has the strongest contrast of the group.

**Can I use these on Codex CLI?**
Pink Custom targets the Dream Skin engine and Sakura Pink Dawn comes from the Cidala Tiger repo; Hashimoto Arata is a Codex Desktop preset. For the CLI, a mono or light skin usually fits the terminal better.

# 最佳柔和粉彩 Codex 皮肤：温柔配色也好读

柔和粉彩（pastel-soft）Codex 皮肤把刺眼的高对比换成低饱和的温柔色。如果你想要一个安静的工作区，又受不了纯白的晃眼或纯黑的沉闷，这篇就是给你看的。我把皮肤库里三款 pastel-soft 都试了一遍，挑出真正能撑住长时间写代码的。最好的柔和粉彩 Codex 皮肤，既好看又有足够对比，文字不会糊。

## 为什么选粉彩皮肤

粉彩皮肤不是简单地"弄成粉色"。好的那几款会在柔化背景的同时，保留语法高亮的区分度。我喜欢在深夜用，因为亮色主题像盯着灯。它们截图也好看，爱晒桌面的人会懂。

## 三款粉彩精选

### 1. Hashimoto Arata
带玫瑰色调的柔和亮色主题。Fei-Away 把它做成机器测试的预设，高光和阴影都平衡。想要不刺眼的亮色模式，它是稳妥的默认。

### 2. Pink Custom
为 Codex Dream Skin 引擎深夜写代码做的柔和粉红渐变。它偏梦幻，但编辑器框架仍然清楚。晚上写东西，这款眼睛很放松。

### 3. Sakura Pink Dawn
来自 Codex Cidala Tiger Skin 仓库的柔和樱花粉抽象预设（MIT）。三款里最有装饰感，用的是淡淡的抽象纹理，而不是平涂。

## 粉彩也能很专注

颜色柔和不代表设置也软。快捷键、AI 助手、多文件导航都原样保留，皮肤只改"看"。不少人白天用粉彩桌面皮肤，夜里换 mono 或暗色，一条命令切换。

## 常见问题

**柔和粉彩 Codex 皮肤安装安全吗？**
安全。它们只是改配色和窗口质感的皮肤文件，不碰你的代码，也不跑脚本。

**粉彩皮肤会影响可读性吗？**
对比太低才会。上面三款都保留了语法区分度。若某款显得发灰，调低屏幕亮度，或选对比最强的 Hashimoto Arata。

**这些能在 Codex CLI 用吗？**
Pink Custom 面向 Dream Skin 引擎，Sakura Pink Dawn 来自 Cidala Tiger 仓库；Hashimoto Arata 是 Codex Desktop 预设。CLI 端通常用 mono 或亮色皮肤更搭。
