A good dark theme does more than look cool — it reduces eye strain, keeps contrast comfortable at night, and helps you focus by hiding the browser-chrome clutter. Here are the best dark Codex skins in the index, ranked by installs and mood.

## 1. Gothic Void Expedition — the built-in default

The engine's default preset proves dark doesn't mean lifeless. A solemn cathedral-horizon palette with deep blue-blacks, it's the safest choice if you want a polished dark look without hunting for a preset.

```text
Codex, switch my workspace to the 'Gothic Void Expedition' preset from Codex Dream Skin — a solemn cathedral-horizon dark sci-fi theme.
```

- **Mood:** Dark & Midnight · solemn
- **Installs:** 2,100+
- [View skin](/skins/gothic-void-expedition/)

## What makes a dark Codex skin "good"?

- **Contrast without glare:** dark background, but text luminance kept high enough for readability.
- **Accent discipline:** one or two accent colors (teal, magenta, amber) used sparingly.
- **Comfort at night:** avoid pure #000 — slightly tinted blacks reduce halation on OLED and IPS alike.
